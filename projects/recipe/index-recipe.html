Introduction

Yeilds 6 buns
Total Time: ~4 Hours

More information about Strange Buns can be found here. Gus occasianlly serves this dish at the Stardrop Saloon.

Ingredients(buns)

  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • 2 cups bread flour
  • 3 tablesspoons unsalted butter
  • 3 tablespoons malted milk powder
  • 2 tablespoons dark brown sugar
  • Kosher salt
  • 1 packet instant yeast
  • 1/2 cup buttermilk (room temperature)
  • 2 large eggs (room temperature)
  • 1 cup all purpose flour
  • 2 whole lobsters
  • 1/2 cup salted butter
  • Freshy ground black pepper
  • 6 large sage leaves, thinly sliced
  • 2 teaspoons lemon zest
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • Equipment: 6 cup JUMBO muffin tin and 6 wooden skewers

Instructions(buns)

  1. In a medium saucepan whisk whole milk and 3 tablespoons of the breadflower until smooth
  2. Place the saucepan over low heat and cook the mix while whisking constantly until thick
  3. Transfer the milk mixture to a small bowl and let cool until warm
  4. Lightly grease a large bowl with butter
  5. In another large bowl whisk together malted milk powder, brown sugar, 2 teaspoons of salt, yeast, buttermilk, and 1 egg until well mixed
  6. Add remaining bread flour, all purpose flour, and the milk mixture
  7. Use a rubber spatula to combine into shaggy dough
  8. Use hands to completely mix the rest until dough is smooth
  9. Add 3 tablespoons butter, 1 tablespoon at a time, using your hands to mix before adding more
  10. Knead dough until no longer wet and sticky
  11. Turn out dough on clean and flat surface and continue kneading until smooth and stretchy
  12. Shape into ball and placed in greased bowl, turning it to cover it in grease
  13. Cover and let dough rise at room temperature for about 2 hours until puffed to twice its size
  14. Lightly grease the cups of your muffin tin
  15. Deflate dough gently and press into rectangle. Then divide into 6 equal pieces
  16. Roll each piece into a 16 inch rope about 1/2 inch thick
  17. Starting at one end roll each rope into a semi-conical spiral shape with a larger base and 2 coils that get slightly smaller as you work up
  18. Tuck second end of dough into the top of the bun and place buns into muffin cups
  19. Place skewers pointed side up into the center of each bun
  20. Let buns rest uncovered until puffy and squishy, about 1 hour
  21. Make sure the buns are standing upright, strange shape is okay
  22. Preheat oovern to 350 C
  23. In a small bowl whisk the remaining egg and a splash of water
  24. Brush egg wash over buns
  25. Bake buns until golden brown, about 30 minutes
  26. Rotate the baking sheet after 20 minutes
  27. Transfer buns to wire rack to cool completely

Ingredients(void mayo)

  • 5 black garlic gloves
  • 1/4 teaspoon food-grade finely ground activated charcoal powder
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 2 teaspoons Dijon mustard
  • Kosher salt
  • 3/4 cup neutral oil, such as light olive oil

Instructions(void mayo)

  1. Use back of large knife to smash garlic into paste and transfer to small bowl
  2. In a medium bowl, combine activated charcoal and 2 teaspoons of lemon juice. Whisk until fully dissolved
  3. Add eeg yolk, Diijon mustard, and a large pinch of salt, then whisk again
  4. Slowly drizzle oil into bowl and continue to whisk
  5. When half of oil is left start to add faster until gone
  6. When mixture is thick and creamy, remain remaining teaspon of lemon juice, a splash of water, and the garlic paste
  7. (Optional)Add more water if too thick. Add salt to taste

Instructions(assembly)

  1. Remove wooden skewers and use a small knife to make rougly 2-inch openings at the top of each bun, then hoolow out the bun with hands
  2. Loosely cover buns
  3. (Optional):use left over bun for bread crumbs
  4. Brings a large pot of salted water to a boil over medium heat. Fill an extra-large bowl with ice water and season with salt
  5. Add lobsters dry to pot, cover with lid, and boil until shells are bright red and meat is cooked, about 8 to 10 minutes
  6. Transfer lobsters directly to ice bath to cool
  7. Pat lobsters dry with towel
  8. Remove meat from claws, tails, and legs, then roughly chop into bite-sized chucks; you should have about 2 1/2 cups
  9. Chill lobster meat
  10. In a medium saucepan melt salted butter, add several large grinds of black pepper and cook stirring often for about 7 to 10 minutes
  11. Turn off heat and add sage, lemon zest, and grated garlic, and warm for 1 minute
  12. Add lobster and stir to combine in the warm butter sauce. Season with additional salt and pepper if needed
  13. Divide the lobster mixture amoung the 6 hollowed-out buns
  14. Drizzle any extra butter sauce over the lobster and serve immediately, accompained by the void mayonnaise

Notes and Variations

This dish is best served fresh. Alternates can be used for lobster; use plums or crawfish.
