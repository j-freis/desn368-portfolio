History

The history of this unique treat is scattered and altered through time and the conflict between humans and monsters. Most credit the underground dwelling monsters, such as goblins and shadow people, in greating this dish originally with cave crawfish in place of lobster. When the recipe made its way to the surface humans altered the original recipe to make it more attractive and lessen the trips to the mining caves for the crawfish. it is now a popular 'dare dish' that young people will dare each other to each because of its interesting ingredients and name.

Where People Make It

This recipe has been documented being used in monster communities, a popular dish of the shadow people. it has recently been brought and made to Pelican Town in Stardew Valley and remains the most prominent location. Zuzu City does have a few unique food locations that make their own more universally appealing versions.

Variations

A vegetarian variation, or a sweeter variation, can be made with plums in place of lobster. The original crawfish recipe can also be used, though more crawfish are needed compared to the number of lobsters due to the size difference. MORE VARATIONS COMING SOON!

Sources

Shane claims to have found this recipe in a magezine and is the only townsfolk in Pelican Town to enjoy the meal. Local shadowperson, Krobus, has stated that he finds nothing "strange" with the bun and enjoys it greatly. This recipe is most credibly sited at StardewValleyWiki.com.
